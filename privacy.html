Privacy Policy – AliAutoPoster

Last updated: September 30, 2026

AliAutoPoster is a private automation tool operated for the Facebook Page Mega Deal Express.

What the app does

The app uses Meta's Pages API to create and schedule product posts on the Mega Deal Express Facebook Page.

Information we process

The app processes only the Page information and permissions needed to manage posts on the Page. It does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information from people who view, like, comment on, or click the Page's posts.

Affiliate links may lead to AliExpress. Any information provided on AliExpress is governed by AliExpress's own privacy policy and terms.

Data retention

The app stores basic publishing records, such as product identifiers, post text, publication time, and Facebook post identifiers, for operating the Page and avoiding duplicate posts. It does not store Facebook passwords or personal data belonging to Page visitors.
