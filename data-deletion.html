User Data Deletion Instructions – AliAutoPoster

AliAutoPoster does not collect or store personal information from people who view, like, comment on, or click posts on the Mega Deal Express Facebook Page.

If you believe information connected to you has been processed by the app, or you want to request deletion, email megadealxpress@gmail.com with the subject Data Deletion Request and describe the information or Facebook interaction concerned.

We will review the request and delete any applicable information under our control within 30 days. Information held by Facebook, AliExpress, sellers, or other independent services must be requested directly from those services.
